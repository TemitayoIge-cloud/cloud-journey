# CLOUD FOR GRANDMA (in 10 lines)

#1. Grandma, think about the library you may have visited as ayoung woman.
#2. You don't buy every book you want to read. That would cost too much and fill your house
#3. The library keeps thousands of books in one big uilding for everyone.
#4. You walk in, borrow the one you need, and take it home.
#5. When you are done, you return it, and someone else can borrow it.
#6. Cloud Computing works the same way, buy with computer storage and power instead of books.
#7. Big companies keep huge collections of computers, like a giant library, far away.
#8. You “borrow” what you need through your phone or tablet, whether it is photos or videos or messages 
#9. You don’t have to own, store or look after any yourself.
#10. So the cloud is a big shared library of computer power, and everyone borrows only what they need.

#And just like the librarian keeps the books safe and in order, the cloud company keeps your things safe, so you don’t have to worry.
