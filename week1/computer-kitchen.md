# EXPLAINING CPU, RAM, AND STORAGE (using a kitchen)

# 1. CPU = The cook. The Cook does the actual work i.e chopping, stirring, following the recipe
# one step at a time. A faster cook, or more cooks gets more done at once.

# 2. RAM: The Counter. It's the workspace where the ingredients ben used at the moment are laid out. A
# a bigger counter lets the cook juggle more dishes without stopping to fetch things. But when the kitchen gets
# gets closed (power off), the counter gets wiped clean.

# 3. STORAGE: THE FRIDGE/PANTRY. It holds everything you own, whetger you're using it or not
# It's big and keeps thimgs for the long haul, even when the lights are off but it is slower.
# The cook has to walk over, grab ingredients, and bring them to the counter first.

# How they work together: You open an app (a recipe), the ingredients move from the fridge to the counter, and the cook
# gets to work. If the counter is too small, the cook keeps running back and forth to the fridge, and everything slows down.
# That is whh adding RAM can make a sluggish computer feel faster.
